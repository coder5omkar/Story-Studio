## Story Studio settings per video

1. Create a project with the title and premise.
2. Story instructions: write the genre, tone, key relationships and any themes
   to avoid. For a pure social/romantic story, add:
   `No financial subplot, no EMI, no loans, no salary or savings details.`
3. Story: generate, inspect checks, approve.
4. Scene plan: generate, **Restyle for current style**, approve.
5. Sample Studio:
   - Voice: preview spoken text, approve.
   - Image: one sample, approve.
   - Music: optional; disable it in Settings if not wanted.
   - Preview clip: render with subtitles on/off, approve.
6. Production: images, music (optional), voice, assembly.
7. Final review: watch, approve, download `final.mp4`.

## YouTube upload checklist

- **File:** 1920×1080 MP4, H.264 + AAC
- **Thumbnail:** 1280×720, 3–5 Hindi words, one strong emotion, high contrast
- **Title:** `कहानी का नाम | Hindi Moral Story | Osho Seekhe`
- **Description:** 2-line hook, story summary without spoilers, moral, disclaimer,
  channel links and business email
- **Tags:** use the channel keywords plus story-specific words
- **End screen:** 10 seconds; link to the next story
- **Pinned comment:** ask a question about the story’s decision
- **Shorts:** cut 30–45 second emotional moments with subtitles
- **Community post:** poll or question the day before upload
