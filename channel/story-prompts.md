# Osho Seekhe — Story Studio Prompts

Use these prompts one project at a time. Recommended baseline:

- **Length:** 4–5 minutes
- **Chapters:** 5
- **Story model:** `deepseek-v4.1-flash`
- **Image style:** Indian flat-vector (primary) or Watercolor storybook (emotional specials)
- **Voice:** Chatterbox Hindi; preview spoken text before approving
- **Music:** optional; disable it if you want a clean narration

For stories that should not contain a financial subplot, the instruction is
written explicitly. You can edit any prompt before generating.

---

## 1. कागज़ का घर

**Style:** Watercolor storybook  
**Thumbnail text:** अधूरा प्रेम

**Topic**

```text
Two lovers, Aarav and Meera, are separated by harsh social and family pressure.
Meera is forced into a wealthy marriage; Aarav is left heartbroken. Years later,
Meera secretly visits him in pain, and the truth about the forced marriage comes out.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Romantic tragedy. Focus on love, social pressure, dignity and emotional pain.
No financial subplot, no EMI, no loans, no salary or savings details.
End with a quiet, bittersweet closure rather than a filmy reunion.
```

---

## 2. आखिरी पत्र

**Style:** Indian flat-vector  
**Thumbnail text:** पिता का आखिरी पत्र

**Topic**

```text
After their father's death, two brothers divide the family house and stop speaking.
Months later, the younger brother finds a letter their father wrote before dying.
The letter reveals a sacrifice the elder brother hid for years.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Family drama about forgiveness and inherited misunderstanding.
Keep the conflict emotional, not financial. End with a partial, believable reconciliation.
```

---

## 3. माँ का हिसाब

**Style:** Watercolor storybook  
**Thumbnail text:** माँ का छुपा हिसाब

**Topic**

```text
A successful son returns home after his mother's death and finds an old notebook.
In it, she recorded every small sacrifice she made for the family, none of which
she ever mentioned. He realizes how much of his life was quietly paid for by her.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Emotional family story about gratitude and invisible labour.
Avoid melodrama; use small domestic details. End with the son changing one habit.
```

---

## 4. वो पुरानी साइकिल

**Style:** Indian flat-vector  
**Thumbnail text:** पिता की साइकिल

**Topic**

```text
A successful young man is embarrassed when his father arrives at his office on an
old bicycle. A colleague laughs. Later he learns that the bicycle paid for his
education and that his father still rides it to save money.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Father-son story about shame, dignity and gratitude. Keep the ending restrained.
No financial-advice lesson; the lesson is about respect.
```

---

## 5. शहर की छत

**Style:** Watercolor storybook  
**Thumbnail text:** छत पर बसा सपना

**Topic**

```text
A migrant worker lives with his daughter on a city rooftop. He tells her the rooftop
is a palace and saves every rupee for her school admission. A storm damages their
shelter the night before the admission test.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Story about parental sacrifice, hope and urban loneliness.
Focus on the father-daughter relationship. End with a small act of help from an unexpected neighbour.
```

---

## 6. दो नाम एक छत

**Style:** Indian flat-vector  
**Thumbnail text:** एक छत, दो परिवार

**Topic**

```text
Two families share one rented house. A missing utensil turns into a daily war of
words. When a child falls ill, both families must cooperate for one night.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Neighbourhood drama about empathy and listening. Keep it light, warm and human.
No villains; both sides are partly right.
```

---

## 7. सबसे महंगी चाय

**Style:** Indian flat-vector  
**Thumbnail text:** ईमानदारी की कीमत

**Topic**

```text
A tea seller finds a bag of money left behind by a rich customer. His family needs
the money urgently, but he waits at the stall until the customer returns. The
customer learns that honesty is not a transaction.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Moral story about integrity and quiet self-respect. Avoid a preachy tone.
Show the temptation honestly, then the choice.
```

---

## 8. चुप्पी का दाम

**Style:** Graphic novel  
**Thumbnail text:** अब बोलो

**Topic**

```text
A woman has stayed silent for years to keep her family together. At a family
gathering, a younger cousin is about to face the same pressure. She finally speaks.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Social drama about courage, voice and protecting the next generation.
Handle the subject with sensitivity; no graphic violence. End with the family
forced to listen, not with an unrealistic fairytale resolution.
```

---

## 9. एक दिन की सैलरी

**Style:** Watercolor storybook  
**Thumbnail text:** एक दिन की मजदूरी

**Topic**

```text
A daily-wage worker is denied payment for one day's work. That day's wage is the
difference between medicine for his child and an empty night. A young supervisor
must decide whether to follow the register or the human being in front of him.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Story about fairness, dignity and small decisions. Avoid a financial-advice lesson.
End with the supervisor making a costly but decent choice.
```

---

## 10. बारिश के बाद

**Style:** Indian flat-vector  
**Thumbnail text:** बारिश के बाद

**Topic**

```text
A farmer loses his crop in unseasonal rain. The village must decide whether to help
him replant or protect their own stores. An old farmer remembers a debt of kindness
from twenty years ago.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Rural story about community, memory and resilience. Keep the language simple.
No policy lecture; show community through action.
```

---

## 11. नई सुबह

**Style:** Anime / hand-painted  
**Thumbnail text:** दोबारा शुरू करो

**Topic**

```text
A student fails an important exam in front of everyone and decides to give up.
A retired teacher gives him one small task: teach three younger children for a week.
Through teaching, he finds a reason to try again.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Comeback story about failure, self-worth and second chances. Avoid a miracle rank;
end with him starting again, not winning everything.
```

---

## 12. दीये की लौ

**Style:** Watercolor storybook  
**Thumbnail text:** आखिरी दीया

**Topic**

```text
On Diwali night, a poor lamp maker has one diya left. He gives it to a stranger
sitting alone outside the market. Years later, that stranger returns with a way
to help the lamp maker's family.
```

**Story instructions**

```text
Warm festival story about kindness and unexpected returns. Keep it grounded;
the return should feel human, not magical.
```
