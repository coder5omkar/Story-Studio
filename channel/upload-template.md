# Osho Seekhe — Upload Template

## Title formula

```text
[कहानी का शीर्षक] | Hindi Emotional Story | Osho Seekhe
```

Examples:

```text
कागज़ का घर | Hindi Emotional Story | Osho Seekhe
आखिरी पत्र | Hindi Family Story | Osho Seekhe
माँ का हिसाब | Hindi Moral Story | Osho Seekhe
```

## Description template

```text
[1–2 lines that create curiosity without spoiling the ending.]

इस कहानी में देखिए: [relationships / struggle / love / social pressure / courage].

कहानी की सीख: [one sentence moral].

Disclaimer: This is a fictional story. No real person, bank, company or brand is represented. This content is for storytelling and educational purposes only.

नई कहानी हर मंगलवार और शुक्रवार शाम 7 बजे।

SUBSCRIBE: [channel link]
Business: oshosheekhe@gmail.com

#HindiKahani #MoralStory #EmotionalStory #OshoSeekhe
```

## Thumbnail formula

- 1280×720
- One face or one strong object
- 3–5 Hindi words maximum
- High contrast: dark ink-blue + amber/cream
- Channel mark in a corner
- No more than one small secondary line

Post 1–2 Shorts on Saturday and a community poll on Sunday. Do not publish all
12 at once; consistency matters more than volume.

## Upload checklist

- [ ] Story approved in Story Studio
- [ ] Scene plan approved
- [ ] Voice sample approved; spoken text preview checked
- [ ] Image sample approved
- [ ] Music approved or disabled
- [ ] Preview clip approved with the intended subtitle setting
- [ ] Final video approved
- [ ] Thumbnail exported at 1280×720
- [ ] Title, description, tags and pinned comment filled
- [ ] Disclaimer included
- [ ] End screen links to one previous story
- [ ] Shorts cut from the best 30–45 seconds
- [ ] Community post scheduled for the day before upload
